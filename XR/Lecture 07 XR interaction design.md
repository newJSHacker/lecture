## 1. UX

VR UI is not a 2D website pasted at 1 m without thought. Prefer world panels at arm's length, large hit targets.

## 2. Fatigue

Arms-up is tiring. Waist-level menus. Rest poses.

## 3. Feedback

Highlight, sound optional, haptic optional.
