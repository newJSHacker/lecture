## 1. IGWT angle

This is **AI for interactive graphics**: APIs, images-as-textures, limits of 3D generation, agents that drive a scene, latency, evaluation — not a ML theory degree and not 'make a startup in 15 weeks'.

## 2. Integrity

Students may use AI tools as in the handbook. They must label generated assets. Exams remain human.

## 3. Harm

No CSAM, no non-consensual deepfakes, no medical advice as a product claim.
