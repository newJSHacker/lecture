## Learning goals

1. Form teams (2–4) or justified solo.
2. Write a problem, not a tech list.
3. Name users.
4. Don't start in Three.js before the problem.
5. Conflict plan.

---

## 1. Capstone

A complete interactive application: configurator, viz, museum, game prototype, walkthrough, medical **viz** (not a device), creative demo. [[01 subjects.md]] list.

## 2. Problem

'We will use R3F and AI' is not a problem. 'A visitor must compare three bronze statues on a phone' is.

## 3. Teams

Roles: graphics, UI, assets, producer. Git hygiene from day 1.
